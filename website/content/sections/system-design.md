A playbook for the 45-minute design interview, the numbers you must be able to estimate, twenty-nine one-page concept sheets (each with a "Say this" line and the trade-offs), and six full walkthroughs. The Digital Mansion primer is the gentle on-ramp if you have never been asked to "design Instagram".

## Playbook
The framework, the numbers, SOLID in JavaScript, and the house-building metaphor for your first design question.

## Concepts
One page per concept: what it is, how it works, the trade-offs, the numbers, and the sentence to say.

## Walkthroughs
Six systems end to end: requirements, estimates, API, data model, high-level design, deep dives, bottlenecks.
