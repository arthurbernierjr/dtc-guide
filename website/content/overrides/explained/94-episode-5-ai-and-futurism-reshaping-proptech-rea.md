# Episode 5: AI and Futurism: Reshaping PropTech & Real Estate

The episode uses companies such as Airbnb, Zillow, and Redfin to open a broader conversation about technology in real estate. Different products address different parts of the experience: discovery, information, booking, transactions, or ongoing management.

That breadth matters because “disrupting real estate” is too vague to explain a product. A renter trying to understand a listing, a manager coordinating repairs, and an owner comparing information face different problems. A useful tool needs to know whose problem it is solving.

## Follow a repair from report to resolution

Consider a tenant reporting a leak. A digital system might collect details, route the request, schedule access, and record the outcome. The value comes from helping the right people act, not merely producing a more polished ticket.

Now add sensors or automated analysis. The product needs a reason for the information it collects and a clear explanation of who can access it. A building is also someone's living environment, so convenience and monitoring can be experienced very differently by different participants.

Use the episode to map one property-related workflow. Identify who supplies information, who makes decisions, and who experiences the consequences. That map helps you judge where AI or another technology could improve the work and where a better process or clearer communication might matter more.
