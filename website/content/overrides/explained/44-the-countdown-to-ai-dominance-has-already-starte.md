# The Countdown to AI Dominance Has Already Started

The countdown framing gives this conversation its urgency. Questions about superior machine intelligence, morality, and control can feel enormous, especially when science fiction supplies such vivid images of what might happen.

One way to make the discussion concrete is to examine authority in a system you could build today. A model that drafts a response has one kind of responsibility. A model that sends it, spends money, or changes an account has another. The consequences come from the actions the surrounding system permits, as well as from the quality of the model's answer.

## Draw the permission boundary

For an automated guild assistant, you might allow it to summarize public quest descriptions while requiring a person to approve changes to rewards. That boundary can be inspected and tested. “The AI should behave responsibly” is a much less complete design.

The larger forecasts remain worth discussing, but practical preparation can begin before anyone settles their timeline. Learn to evaluate outputs, limit actions, keep records, and design a useful human handoff.

[Binding the Great Spirits](/path/10-3-binding-the-great-spirits/) turns the metaphor into application work. The interesting achievement is giving a powerful tool a clear job and making its behavior understandable enough that someone can remain responsible for the result.
