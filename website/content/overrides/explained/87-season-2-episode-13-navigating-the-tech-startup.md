# Season 2 Episode 13: Navigating The Tech Startup Ecosystem

Ashley brings experience in tech recruitment to a conversation about founding an AI company. That background makes the human side of a startup particularly relevant: recognizing capabilities, choosing collaborators, and helping people work through problems that do not yet have an established process.

Early teams often need people to work across boundaries. A product question may reveal a technical limitation. A customer conversation may force a change in priorities. A hiring decision may determine which problems the company can realistically attempt next.

## Hire for the work in front of you

Suppose a founder says the company needs a senior engineer. Before writing the title, describe the decisions that person must make. Is the challenge building a prototype, making an unreliable system dependable, or helping a small team establish a workable development process? Those are related but different needs.

The same clarity helps evaluate collaboration. How does a person explain uncertainty? Can they ask for missing context? How do they respond when evidence challenges their preferred solution? A startup needs those habits alongside technical ability.

The episode also touches funding and growth, but the team connects those topics to execution. Money can increase the available options; people still have to choose among them and deliver. Bring one current project problem to the conversation and ask what capability, responsibility, or working relationship would actually help resolve it.
