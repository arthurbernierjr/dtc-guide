# Season 2 Episode 6 DeFi Drama: Bankers Beware?

“Bankers beware” frames DeFi as a challenge to familiar financial institutions. The deeper question is which functions a system changes. Holding assets, moving them, matching borrowers with lenders, and deciding how disputes are resolved are different jobs, even when an application presents them through one interface.

Begin with a transaction you can trace. Someone connects a wallet, supplies an asset, and receives a claim on something in return. Where is the asset now? What authorizes its movement? What must happen for the person to withdraw it again?

## Ask where the return comes from

A displayed percentage is the beginning of an explanation. The next questions concern its source: payments from borrowers, trading fees, newly distributed tokens, or another mechanism. These sources can behave differently, and an attractive number alone does not explain whether it can persist.

The same care applies to vocabulary. Mining, staking, and lending describe different activities; using them interchangeably makes it harder to understand what a participant is actually doing.

This episode is a starting point for investigating the architecture of a financial service, rather than a reason to choose an investment. Sketch the participants and label what each controls. If you cannot explain a step without saying “the platform handles it,” that is the step to investigate next. The word decentralized should make you curious about the distribution of control, not end that inquiry.
