# S3 E4 How to Build Wealth & Empower Communities with FinTech and Credit with Haziq Ali

Haziq Ali's conversation brings together Novae, credit education, marketing, and community. It highlights a product challenge that is easy to underestimate: access to an interface does not necessarily give someone an understanding of the process behind it.

A person may see a score, a status, or an automated recommendation without knowing what information produced it or what they can do next. Clear explanations and a workable path for questions are part of the service, not decorations added after the software is finished.

## Design for the moment a customer is confused

Imagine a user who believes something in their information is incorrect. Can they see what the system is using? Is the next step understandable? Can they reach someone who can help, and can they tell whether their request has progressed?

These questions become particularly important when automation is introduced. Faster processing is useful only in relation to the quality and consequences of the decisions being processed.

Community can support learning by giving people language for problems they thought were theirs alone. It also creates a responsibility to explain offers and expectations carefully. Listen for how education, product design, and customer support fit together in the business. The strongest version of empowerment helps a person make a more informed decision, including when they decide an offer is not right for them.
