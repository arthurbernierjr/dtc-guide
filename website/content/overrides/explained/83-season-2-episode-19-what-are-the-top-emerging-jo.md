# Season 2 Episode 19: What Are The Top Emerging Jobs in The New Economy

The episode looks toward roles shaped by changes such as AI and green energy. A list of titles can help you notice unfamiliar possibilities, but titles alone reveal little about what someone does on an ordinary workday.

Start with the work beneath the label. Does the role involve investigating data, maintaining equipment, coordinating a deployment, explaining a product, or understanding a specialized domain? Different titles may share capabilities, while identical titles at different organizations may describe quite different jobs.

## Build a bridge from a skill you already have

Imagine a support specialist interested in AI-related work. Their experience with recurring customer problems may be valuable when evaluating an automated support system. They would still need to learn the system's limitations and how to assess results, but they are not starting without relevant knowledge.

A useful exploration project connects a current strength with a new demand. Interview someone doing the work, study a real task, and create a small artifact that demonstrates your understanding of it. The artifact might be an analysis, a prototype, a process improvement, or a clear explanation.

Treat the episode as a map of possibilities rather than a live hiring forecast. Opportunities vary by place, organization, and time. The durable habit is investigating what a role actually requires and building evidence that you can contribute to that work.
