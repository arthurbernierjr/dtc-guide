# This Gold Rush Won't Wait for You to Learn Everything

AI, blockchain, and the creator economy each contain opportunities, but placing the three labels together does not yet describe a business. The productive intersection is a specific job that becomes easier, cheaper, or newly possible for a particular person.

Imagine a creator who spends hours turning a long interview into useful lesson notes. A small tool that produces a reviewable draft addresses recognizable work. Adding a token or a marketplace would need a separate reason. Every technology should earn its place in the user's experience.

## Start before mastery; finish something narrow

Waiting to know an entire field can become a comfortable way to avoid exposing your work to judgment. At the other extreme, racing to announce a company can substitute activity for learning. A bounded experiment gives you a third path: one user, one task, one result you can inspect.

Decide what would make the experiment worth continuing. Did someone use the draft? How much correction did it require? Would they return without a reminder? Those questions reveal more than whether the demo looks futuristic.

[The first capstone sprint](/path/12-1-sprint-1-foundation/) practices this kind of completeness at a small scale. The opportunity is to become capable through real attempts, while keeping each attempt understandable enough that you can learn from its outcome.
