# Big Poppa Code | The Integrity Response w/ CEO Khacki #142

Arthur's appearance on The Integrity Response sits within CEO Khacki's broader interest in passion, craft, ambition, consciousness, and integrity. Those themes offer a useful lens for a technical career because skill alone does not determine how someone handles responsibility.

It is easy to describe a standard when nothing is at stake. The revealing moment arrives when following it costs time, status, or an opportunity. A deadline may tempt someone to conceal a defect. A public success may tempt them to take more credit than their contribution warrants.

## Look at the decision before the explanation

Imagine discovering that a feature you presented as complete does not handle an important case. The next action matters: clarifying the limitation, proposing a repair, and letting others make informed decisions about the release. A polished explanation after the fact cannot replace that response.

Bring your own example to the conversation: a time when the result you wanted and the standard you valued pulled in different directions. What did you do, who was affected, and what would you handle differently with the understanding you have now?

For a learner, integrity can begin in ordinary habits. Describe what you understand, give credit for help, and make uncertainty visible early enough that someone can respond. Those habits create a foundation for greater responsibility long before a job title announces that you have it.
