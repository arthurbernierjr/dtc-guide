# Forgotten Prospects? AI Knows How to Win Them Back

A dormant lead is a person whose circumstances may have changed. Perhaps the timing was wrong, the offer was unclear, or another problem took priority. The episode's AI-assisted reactivation idea becomes interesting when automation helps recover that context instead of merely increasing the volume of follow-up.

A useful message might reconnect a previous need with a genuinely new development: the requested integration now exists, a service area has expanded, or an earlier limitation has been removed. “Just checking in” gives the recipient much less to work with.

## Make the handoff part of the system

The difficult moment is often the reply. Someone asks a question, objects to a detail, or says the need has disappeared. The system needs to recognize those differences and bring a person into conversations that require judgment. It also needs a clear way to honor an opt-out.

A commission-based arrangement adds another design problem: attribution. Agree on what counts as a generated sale, how existing conversations are handled, and what happens with refunds. Otherwise a successful campaign can still create an argument about the result.

For builders, this is a compact product lesson. Good automation includes the records it reads, the messages it may send, the exceptions it hands over, and the outcome it measures. The language model is one part of that workflow.
