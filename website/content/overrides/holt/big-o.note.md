**Read this with Session 9.2.** This lesson says "look for loops", and that is the right first habit. One addition from [Session 9.2](/path/9-2-trial-of-blades/): a loop can hide inside a method. `includes`, `indexOf`, `find` and `splice` each walk the array for you, so a call to one of them inside your own loop is a loop inside a loop, O(n²), even though you only typed one `for`. Count the loops you can see, then count the ones a method runs for you.
